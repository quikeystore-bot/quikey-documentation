# Custom Domain Setup

Connecting a custom domain to your Quikey store allows you to build brand identity and provide a professional shopping experience for your customers.

## Step 1: Add your domain in Quikey

1. Go to your store's **Settings** dashboard and navigate to **Domains**.
2. Enter your custom domain (e.g. `yourstore.com`) in the "Connect a custom domain" input box.
3. Click the **Connect** button.
4. Your domain will now appear in the Connected Domains list with a **Pending Verification** status.

## Step 2: Add DNS records to your provider

To connect your domain, you need to prove ownership and route traffic to Quikey's servers. 

Click on the **Verify** button next to your pending domain in the Quikey dashboard. You will be provided with two CNAME records. 

Log in to your domain provider (e.g., Hostinger, Namecheap, GoDaddy) and add the following records in your DNS settings:

### 1. Traffic Routing (Required)
This record ensures that when customers visit your domain, they are directed to your Quikey store.

- **Type:** `CNAME`
- **Name / Host:** `@` *(or `www` if you are connecting a subdomain)*
- **Value / Target:** `quikey.store`

### 2. SSL Verification (Required)
This record allows Quikey to automatically generate a free SSL certificate for your domain so your store is secure (`https://`).

- **Type:** `CNAME`
- **Name / Host:** `_acme-challenge`
- **Value / Target:** *(Copy the unique Cloudflare value shown in your Quikey dashboard)*

## Step 3: Verify and Go Live!

After adding the DNS records to your provider, wait a few minutes for the changes to propagate across the internet. 

1. Return to your Quikey **Domains** settings.
2. Click the **Check DNS & Verify** button.
3. Once Cloudflare verifies the records, your domain status will change to **Connected**.

Congratulations! Your store is now live on your custom domain.
